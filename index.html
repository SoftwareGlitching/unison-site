One assistant, three ways to work

Switch modes with a tap. Everything happens on your computer.

💬 Chat

Ask questions, plan features, learn. Strong at Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, C#, Lua & Roblox Luau, and Blender's Python API.

⚙️ Agent

It reads and edits files in folders you approve, runs commands, and fixes its own errors — showing you a diff and asking before each change.

🖥️ Screen

A vision model sees your screen and uses the mouse and keyboard to open apps, click, and type. Press Esc any time to stop.

📚 Knowledge

A built-in library of programming notes, plus your own docs and project code, searched while it answers.

🌙 Background jobs

Give it a task and close the tab — it keeps working, and you approve steps from your phone over a private connection.

🔒 Private by design

Runs offline on open-weight models. Password sign-in, every file change backed up, dangerous commands blocked.

Up and running in minutes

A first-run setup downloads a model that fits your hardware and builds the app for you.

Download & unzip

Grab the zip and extract it somewhere with space, like C:\UNISON.

Run Setup

Double-click Setup.bat. It installs what's missing and picks a model for your GPU.

Start chatting

Double-click Start-UNISON.bat; UNISON opens in your browser.

Go further

Turn on agent or screen mode, index your projects, or reach it from your phone.

Nothing leaves your computer. UNISON runs open-weight models locally through Ollama. There are no API keys and no cloud calls — the website only hands you the download.

What you need

It adapts to your machine — a bigger graphics card just means a smarter model.

  • Windows 10 or 1164-bit
  • Graphics card (GPU)Recommended · 6 GB+ VRAM for the best models. Runs on CPU too, slower.
  • Memory16 GB RAM or more recommended
  • Disk space~10 GB (app + one model; more for larger models)
  • InternetOnly for the one-time setup & model download
