Setup

FAQ

Good questions

The quick answers.

Is it really private?

Yes. The model runs on your own PC and your conversations are stored there. The website only serves the download and the browser chat page; the browser chat runs its model inside your tab.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. Setup picks a model that fits your hardware. A graphics card with 6 GB+ of VRAM gives the best results, but it also runs on CPU, just slower.

What does my license key do?

It unlocks the Windows app and the browser chat. Time-limited keys stop working when they expire, and a key can be locked to a single PC.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The browser chat works on phones with WebGPU, and with Remote turned on at home you can use your PC's bigger model from anywhere through a private link.

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download the app and a model. After that it works offline.

How do I get a key?

Pick a plan above. After you pay, the key arrives by email.
